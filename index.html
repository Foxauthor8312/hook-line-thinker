HOW TO PLAY

1. PICK A CLUE
Teams take turns selecting a category and point value. A coin toss determines who starts.

2. THINK IT THROUGH
Listen to the clue. Teams may discuss before the team leader responds.

3. ANSWER IN THE FORM OF A QUESTION
The host decides whether the answer is correct.

4. SCORING
Correct answers earn the displayed points. Incorrect answers give the other team a chance.

5. KEEP THE GAME MOVING
The team that answers correctly selects next. If neither team is correct, play passes to the opposing team.

6. SPECIAL CHALLENGES
THINKS MORE: A surprise wager clue. Correct adds the wager; incorrect deducts it. No steal opportunity.
THINKS HARDER: After the board is cleared, both teams secretly wager points and answer the same final question independently. Correct answers add the wager; incorrect answers subtract it.

The team leader gives the official answer. Wagers cannot exceed the team's current score. The host's ruling is final.
